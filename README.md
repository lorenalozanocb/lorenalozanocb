## 👋 About Me

I'm a performance marketer who loves a challenge, with an innovative and curious mindset. For over eight years I've built and run marketing strategies across search, social, programmatic and CRM, for large and mid-sized companies in Canada, the United States and Latin America.

I work where creativity meets data: structuring accounts, testing creative, and turning reporting into decisions that lower acquisition costs and grow qualified demand. Lately I've been bringing AI into that workflow, building tools that make creative QA and testing faster for media teams.

**Currently:** Sr. Paid Social Specialist at **t2ó | North America** (Toronto).
